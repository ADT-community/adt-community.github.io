# Contributing

The full overview is on the website: https://adt-community.github.io

## Quick version

1. Pick a project repo in [ADT-community](https://github.com/ADT-community).
2. Find an issue with a `bounty:$20` label — it's a 2-10 hour task with a 5-day claim window. Comment to claim it.
3. Fork, branch, and open a pull request that links the issue.
4. Once a maintainer merges it, the bounty is paid.

Projects don't earn revenue yet; bounties are funded by the community. What contributors gain is $20 per merged task plus real-world project experience.

**Goals** are bigger milestones (e.g. launch the MVP, first paying customer). The team and each member's share are agreed in the project's issues before work starts, and the reward is paid when the goal is reached.

## Payments

Payouts go by mobile money or bank transfer. Share payment details privately with a maintainer, **never** in public issues.

## Proposing a project

Use the form at https://adt-community.github.io/propose.html, or open an issue in this repo with the **Project proposal** template. A proposal moves forward once it has a clear path to profit, a maintainer and at least 3 contributors.
