# Contributing

The full overview is on the website: https://adt-community.github.io

## Bounties ($20 tasks)

Bounties are short, focused tasks designed to take **2–5 hours** with a fixed **$20 reward**. Open tasks are listed across [ADT-community](https://github.com/ADT-community) project repositories and on [ADT Work](https://adt-community.github.io/work.html).

### How to claim
1. Find an issue labeled `bounty:$20` across any [ADT-community](https://github.com/ADT-community) repo or on [ADT Work](https://adt-community.github.io/work.html).
2. Comment **"I'd like to take this"**.
3. **Start once a maintainer assigns you.**
4. Fork, branch, and open a pull request within **5 days** of being assigned, or the task goes back to the pool.

### Payment
- **$20 in the weekly payout run:** Work merged Monday to Sunday is paid by the following Wednesday to mobile money or bank.
- **Payout details:** After your first merged task you add payout details once, privately with a maintainer (**never** in a public issue or pull request).
- **Payout schedule:** Payouts start in January 2027. You can work now: everything merged before then is recorded and paid in the first payout run on **Wednesday 6 January 2027**, then weekly after that.

### About the reward
ADT projects don't earn money yet. $20 is a thank-you, and the real value is shipping work on a live product that you can show employers. Contributors who keep shipping get first say in revenue sharing once projects earn.

## Goals

**Goals** are bigger milestones (e.g. launch the MVP, first paying customer). The team and each member's share are agreed in the project's issues before work starts, and the reward is paid when the goal is reached (via mobile money or bank transfer).

## Proposing a project

Use the form at https://adt-community.github.io/propose.html, or open an issue in this repo with the **Project proposal** template. A proposal moves forward once it has a clear path to profit, a maintainer and at least 3 contributors.
