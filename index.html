How it works

Find work, claim it, get paid. All on this site.

ADT creates jobs in Africa through deep tech and innovation. Tasks, claims, deliveries and payouts are handled here on ADT Work, for people living in Africa. The code itself is open on GitHub.

3

Get paid

Next wave

Open now, paid as funding grows

Marked Next wave on ADT Work. They become $20 tasks as funding comes in. Take them now for experience and credit on a real product.

  • Same rules as paid tasks
  • Your work is credited publicly
  • Regular contributors get first say in revenue sharing

Weekly payouts: work approved Monday to Sunday is paid by the following Wednesday, by mobile money or bank transfer. The first payout run is on Wednesday 6 January 2027 and covers everything approved before then. You share payout details once, privately, after your first approved task. Never post them in public.

Why $20? ADT projects don't earn money yet. The fee is a thank-you; the bigger value is shipping real work you can show employers, and a share in what these products earn later.

Flagship projects

What we're building.

Four projects to start. Each one solves a daily problem and has a clear way to make money.

01

Minibus app ↗

Uber-style convenience for the minibuses Africa already rides, without owning a single bus.

  • Passengers see minibuses near them on a map with route, fare and arrival time, pay by mobile money and share trips for safety. No booking, just get on.
  • Drivers share GPS and accept fares from their phone.
  • Owners see where each vehicle is, what it earned today and how each driver is doing.

Earns: monthly fee per vehicle, small cut of digital fares, route data for cities.

02

Local-language AI ↗

An AI platform that speaks African languages, by voice and text, for farmers and everyone else.

  • Farming, health, forms, translation, learning and everyday questions
  • Its own app, plus an API other products can build on
  • Reaches basic phones too, through channels like WhatsApp, SMS and voice

Earns: API access for banks, telcos and agri-businesses, partnerships, licensing.

03

Creator social app ↗

Social media that actually pays African creators.

  • Short video and posts built for African audiences
  • Tips, gifts, shopping and brand deals, paid out by mobile money
  • Launch in one or two countries and a niche, then grow

Earns: a cut of tips, gifts and sales, plus brand-deal fees.

04

ADT agent ↗

An AI agent that helps run the whole network of projects.

  • Turns plans into tasks and bounties
  • Monitors progress, reviews and payouts across every repo
  • Flags stalled work, problems and projects that need help

Earns: powers ADT first, then sold to other open source and remote teams.

Community rules

Keep it respectful.

Do

  • Be patient, especially with newcomers
  • Critique work, not people
  • Credit others honestly

Don't

  • Harass or discriminate, including by country, tribe, religion, gender or language
  • Claim others' work or game the reward system
  • Share anyone's private or payment details

Report problems by messaging a maintainer. Moderators can remove content, cancel rewards earned in bad faith, or ban people.
