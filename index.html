Getting paid

Two ways to earn.

The price is agreed before work starts, so there are no surprises and nothing to argue about later.

Bounties

A fixed price for a task

Issues carry a label like bounty:$50. Whoever's pull request gets merged gets paid.

  • Claim the issue first so no one duplicates work
  • Paid after merge
  • Good for features, fixes, designs and docs
Goals

A reward when the project hits a target

Bigger milestones like launch the MVP, first 1,000 users or first paying customer carry a reward for the team.

  • Team and shares agreed at the start
  • Paid when the goal is reached
  • Good for hardware builds and long efforts

Payouts go by mobile money or bank transfer. Payment details are only ever shared privately, never in public issues or discussions.

Every project is a business

Built to make money.

Code is open. Revenue comes from hosting, premium features, fees, licensing and partnerships. Projects with no path to profit get paused, so effort goes where it pays.

Flagship projects

What we're building.

Five projects to start. Each one solves a daily problem and has a clear way to make money.

01

Minibus app ↗

Uber-style convenience for the minibuses Africa already rides, without owning a single bus.

  • Passengers see minibuses near them on a map with route, fare and arrival time, pay by mobile money and share trips for safety. No booking, just get on.
  • Drivers share GPS and accept fares from their phone.
  • Owners see where each vehicle is, what it earned today and how each driver is doing.

Earns: monthly fee per vehicle, small cut of digital fares, route data for cities.

02

Local-language AI ↗

An AI platform that speaks African languages, by voice and text, for farmers and everyone else.

  • Farming, health, forms, translation, learning and everyday questions
  • Its own app, plus an API other products can build on
  • Reaches basic phones too, through channels like WhatsApp, SMS and voice

Earns: API access for banks, telcos and agri-businesses, partnerships, licensing.

03

Creator social app ↗

Social media that actually pays African creators.

  • Short video and posts built for African audiences
  • Tips, gifts, shopping and brand deals, paid out by mobile money
  • Launch in one or two countries and a niche, then grow

Earns: a cut of tips, gifts and sales, plus brand-deal fees.

04

ADT agent ↗

An AI agent that helps run the whole network of projects.

  • Turns plans into tasks and bounties
  • Monitors progress, reviews and payouts across every repo
  • Flags stalled work, problems and projects that need help

Earns: powers ADT first, then sold to other open source and remote teams.

05

Quant trading lab ↗

Open tools for trading African markets with data and code instead of guesswork.

  • Clean price data for exchanges like Johannesburg, Lagos, Nairobi, Accra and the BRVM, plus currencies
  • Backtesting and research tools built for thin, low-volume markets
  • Community-built strategies tested in public, on paper money first

Earns: market-data API, paid research tools, licensing to brokers and fund managers. Trading real capital comes later, only with the right licences.

Community rules

Keep it respectful.

Do

  • Be patient, especially with newcomers
  • Critique work, not people
  • Credit others honestly

Don't

  • Harass or discriminate, including by country, tribe, religion, gender or language
  • Claim others' work or game bounties
  • Share anyone's private or payment details

Report problems with GitHub's "Report content" option or by messaging a maintainer. Moderators can remove content, cancel bounties earned in bad faith, or ban people.
