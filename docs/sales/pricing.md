# ADT Work pricing (partner sales kit)

Single source of truth for the numbers used in the one-pager, on `work.html` and on `partner.html`.
Decision record: [issue #12](https://github.com/ADT-community/adt-community.github.io/issues/12).

## Standard pricing

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Contributor reward per approved task | **$20** (fixed, agreed before work starts) |
| ADT platform fee per approved task | **15%** of the contributor reward = **$3** per $20 task |
| Company pays per approved task | **$23** for a $20 task |
| Payment timing | Invoiced after the work is approved; no charge for work that fails review |

The 15% fee sits inside the 15–20% range suggested in the issue. It is charged only on
approved work and is shown separately from the contributor reward on every quote, so the
company can see exactly what the contributor receives. The contributor reward is unchanged.

## Pilot offer

For the **first two approved tasks**, the platform fee is waived:

- Company pays **$20 per task, $40 total** (instead of $46).
- ADT helps turn the company's brief into a task with a clear "done when" and acceptance test.
- Delivery within **7 days** of assignment; a reviewer checks the work against the acceptance test.
- In return, the company gives a short written review and agrees to be named as a pilot partner.
- Standard 15% pricing applies from the third approved task onward.

The pilot is deliberately small: one brief, one acceptance test, one delivery. It proves the
model without a large commitment, and it gives ADT a reference from a real paying company.

## What a company supplies

1. A one-paragraph brief and a definition of "done" (what a correct result looks like).
2. A named reviewer who can approve or reject within 2 working days.
3. Access to any tools or accounts needed for the task.

## Outreach rules

- The 30-company list in `docs/sales/target-companies.md` is for the ADT team to work through.
- Do not contact companies on ADT's behalf without assignment; do not send bulk email.
- Quote the numbers above verbatim. Any change to pricing must be made in this file first,
  then in the one-pager and both pages, and must be recorded in issue #12.
