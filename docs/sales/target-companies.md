## Suggested outreach order

Start with agencies (13–21): recurring QA, metadata and copy tasks are the easiest first
pilot. Then NGOs (22–30), which often have small backlogs of content cleanup. Fintechs
(1–12) have longer procurement, but a single rejected task costs them little, so lead with
the two-task pilot, not a platform pitch.

Short first note: "We match short, fixed-price tasks to vetted contributors living in Africa.
Your first two approved tasks have no platform fee ($20 each, delivery in 7 days). Would a
brief and an acceptance test for one small task be useful? Full details:
https://adt-community.github.io/partner.html"
