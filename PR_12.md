Closes #12

## Summary

Adds the complete partner sales kit for signing the first company that pays to post tasks on ADT Work: a one-page PDF pitch, a single pricing decision record, a 30-prospect outreach list with public contact routes, and a live partner section on `work.html`.

## Changes

- `docs/sales/one-pager.pdf` — one-page Letter PDF covering vetted African talent, fixed-price tasks, weekly delivery, the pilot flow, pricing and the contact route. Rendered and checked as a single page.
- `docs/sales/generate_one_pager.py` — dependency-free generator so the PDF can be regenerated after a pricing change.
- `.gitattributes` — marks PDFs binary so `core.autocrlf` cannot rewrite the xref offsets.
- `docs/sales/pricing.md` — the numbers used everywhere else: $20 contributor reward, 15% platform fee ($3), $23 per approved task, first two approved tasks with the fee waived ($40 total), nothing charged for rejected work.
- `docs/sales/target-companies.md` — 30 real startups, agencies and NGOs (12 / 9 / 9) with a verified public contact route, region, one plausible first task and a verification note each; includes outreach order and a short first note. The list is for the ADT team; no company was contacted.
- `docs/sales/test_sales_kit.py` — 8 dependency-free checks for the PDF structure/content, pricing consistency across all four files, the 30-row list, both HTML pages and local links.
- `work.html` — new `#partners` section with the three benefits, the price, the pilot offer and links to the PDF and pricing record; added a "For companies" nav link.
- `partner.html` — pricing panel matching `work.html` and links to the kit.
- `README.md` — links to the kit and the check command.
